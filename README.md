### 🌟 About Me
> **"Transforming complex logic into seamless code. Engineering next-generation AI workflows, mobile ecosystems, and high-performance web platforms."**

I am a **Full-Stack Software Engineer, Flutter Specialist & AI Systems Developer** from Sri Lanka. Founder of **Questra** and **Coding Divers**, Co-Founder of **Gravix**. I specialize in developing cross-platform mobile apps (Flutter), scalable web architectures (Next.js 15, FastAPI, Python), and autonomous AI-driven systems.

---

### 🛠️ What I'm Currently Up To
- 🧠 **Autonomous AI Systems:** Architecting intelligent workflow bots, LLM microservices, and AI integrations.
- 📱 **Mobile Engineering:** Building production-grade Flutter applications (ArtimaX, Moni, Solarjade, Sleep Love).
- ⚡ **Cloud & Backend Infrastructure:** High-throughput microservices using Cloudflare Workers, FastAPI, and Next.js.
- 🌍 **Open Source:** Crafting developer tooling, modern web templates, and community packages.
